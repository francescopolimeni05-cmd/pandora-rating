Which story is more unusual for its genre?

This short task is part of a master's thesis at ESADE on why some films stay in collective memory. You will see two plot summaries at a time, both from films of the same genre released around the same year.

Your only job: pick the story that feels more unusual for its genre, that is, less like a typical film of that kind.

  1. Judge only the summaries, not which film sounds better.
  2. There are no right answers, and you don't need to know the films. Please don't look anything up.
  3. If it's close, pick the one that feels even slightly less typical.
  4. Do as many pairs as you like (each takes about 20 seconds) and stop whenever you want. Every answer is saved as soon as you click.

Answers are anonymous: the site stores a random code, your choice and the time taken, nothing else.
